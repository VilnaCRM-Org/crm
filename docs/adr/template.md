## Context and Problem Statement

What is the situation, and what forces make a decision necessary now? State the problem
without naming the answer. Reference the code, config, or measurement that establishes it.

## Decision Drivers

- The constraint, quality attribute, or budget that dominates the choice
- Anything that eliminates an otherwise reasonable option
- Existing repository conventions the decision has to stay consistent with

## Considered Options

1. **Option A** — one-line description.
2. **Option B** — one-line description.

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: **"Option A"**, because … . Name the concrete artifacts that implement the
decision (config keys, gates, modules) so a reader can verify the ADR against the code.

## Positive Consequences

- What gets better, stated so it can be checked

## Negative Consequences

- What gets worse or harder, and the accepted trade-off
- Anything a future reader must not be surprised by
